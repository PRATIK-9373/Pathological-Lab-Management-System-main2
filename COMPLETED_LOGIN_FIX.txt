I have solved the redirection issue by upgrading your login system to be a **Unified Login**.

Now, the "Client Login" page (`login.php`) is smart:
1.  **If you enter Admin credentials**: It detects you are an Admin, logs you in as Admin, and **redirects you to the Admin Dashboard**.
2.  **If you enter Patient credentials**: It detects you are a Patient, logs you in as Client, and redirects you to the Patient Home.

**Changes Made:**
*   **Backend (`classes/Login.php`)**: I updated `client_login` to check the `users` (Admin) table *first*. If it finds a match, it grants Admin access.
*   **Frontend (`login.php`)**: I updated the Javascript to check the login type (`1` for Admin, `2` for Client) and redirect to the correct URL accordingly.

**How to test:**
1.  Go to `http://localhost/Pathological-Lab-Management-System-main/login.php`
2.  Enter your **Admin Username** and **Password** (even though the field says "Email").
3.  Click Sign In.
4.  You should be redirected to the **Admin Dashboard**.
